## Official exam information

- CDMP Data Management Fundamentals: 100 multiple-choice questions, 90-minute standard exam, 110-minute ESL version.
- Published topic weights: Data Management Process 2%, Big Data 2%, Data Architecture 6%, Document and Content Management 6%, Data Ethics 2%, Data Governance 11%, Data Integration and Interoperability 6%, Master and Reference Data Management 10%, Data Modelling and Design 11%, Data Quality 11%, Data Security 6%, Data Storage and Operations 6%, Data Warehousing and Business Intelligence 10%, Metadata Management 11%.
- CDMP certification thresholds: Associate 60% on Fundamentals; Practitioner and Master require 70% or 80% respectively on Fundamentals and two specialist exams, with additional Master experience requirements.

Official pages:

- https://cdmp.info/about/
- https://cdmp.info/faqs/
- https://dama.org/cdmp-frequently-asked-questions/

## Content sources

The initial pilot was authored against:

1. DAMA International, *The DAMA Guide to the Data Management Body of Knowledge*, second edition, Technics Publications, 2017. Freely accessible publisher-hosted PDF: https://technicspub.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/DAMA-DMBOK2.pdf
2. DAMA International, *DAMA-DMBOK2 Revised Edition* public change summary: https://www.damadmbok.org/dmbok2-revisions
3. DAMA International, Revised Edition FAQ: https://dama.org/dama-dmbok2-revised-edition-faqs/

The 2017 PDF is not the current Revised Edition. The public revision summary identifies revision-sensitive changes in Chapters 1, 3, 4 and 13 and in the context diagrams. Those items require verification against the current edition before a release claim.

## Content controls

- Question IDs are stable within this draft.
- `family` identifies the concept tested and will govern future variant and retake-overlap checks.
- Each item stores a topic, subtopic, source locator, edition basis, verification date and draft review status.
- Each item contains original wording and option-specific rationale text. No official exam question or copied book passage is included.
- The pilot is explicitly marked as unofficial, draft and uncalibrated.
